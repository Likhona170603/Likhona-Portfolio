### Agile Team Charter

Our team agreed a charter at the start of the project to set out how we work together.

[Download the Agile Team Charter](Agile-Team-Charter.docx)

# Reflection
 Writing the team charter at the start of the project taught me that good teamwork needs to be planned, not just hoped for. As a team we agreed on how we would communicate (WhatsApp for quick updates, Teams for meetings, email for formal submissions), how fast we would respond, and how we would make decisions when we disagreed. Having the escalation path (consensus, then a vote, then the team leader, then the lecturer) meant that we knew what to do when opinions differed, so disagreements did not slow us down. The Definition of Done was the most useful part for me as the Writer, because it gave us a clear standard for when a Trello card could be ticked off and stopped us from marking work complete too early. The risks section also made us honest about our limits, for example treating payments and messaging as proposed features until they are built and tested. If I did this again, I would review the charter at the end of every sprint, as we planned to, so that it stays up to date with changes in the team and the project. The charter showed me that clear agreements, transparency and accountability are what keep a team working well together.

### Sprint Backlog

Every sprint was split into four day-cards, and each card had a user story and a checklist of subtasks that we ticked off as we went.

[See Sprint Backlog](trello-sprint-1.jpeg,trello-sprint-2.jpeg,trello-sprint-3.jpeg,trello-sprint-4.jpeg,trello-sprint-5.jpeg)

# Reflection
Splitting each sprint into small day-cards made progress easy to see and made blockers easier to spot. Our research sprints (1 to 3) finished with all planned cards ticked off, which showed me how much a clear checklist helps a team stay on track. The two Sprint 1 cards left open taught me that it is better to leave a card honestly open than to mark it done early, and to deal with it at the next planning session.
